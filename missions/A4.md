# A4 — The 07:00 incident

**20 minutes.** This is the other mission that matters most today. It is the one
from minute 0.

## Where we are

2026-03-04's delivery is in. Every task is green. Validation passed.

`daily_revenue` shows **€0.00** for all twelve stores.

This is 2026-03-01 happening again — except this time it is your pipeline.

## What this mission is about

The central lesson of the Airflow half:

> An orchestrator enforces whatever definition of success you give it, and
> supplies none of its own.

Nothing here is a flaw in Airflow. It did exactly what it was told. The question
is what it was told.

## Your task

1. **Run the pipeline for 2026-03-04.** Watch it go green. Look at the number.

2. **Explain why `validate.py` passed.** Read it if you need to. This is not a
   trick — the answer is simple and it is the whole mission.

3. **Make the pipeline fail on this delivery**, using the plausible revenue
   bounds in `config/team.yaml`. `cfg.is_plausible(total)` reads them for you.

4. **Publish the `Asset` event with metadata — only when the data product is
   sound.** The asset is already declared in the stub as `DAILY_REVENUE`.
   Look at the `outlets` argument of `@task`, and at
   `context["outlet_events"][DAILY_REVENUE].extra`.

Step 4 is optional if you run out of time. **Step 3 is not.**

## What success looks like

Before: every task green, €0.
After: the pipeline stops at an explicit semantic gate, with a message naming the
implausible figure *and* the bound it violated.

No asset event for the bad delivery. One *is* emitted, carrying metadata, when a
good delivery is processed.

```
roots verify a4
```

`semantic_gate_enforced` needs **both directions**: your gate must have rejected
the €0 delivery *and* accepted a good one (try 2026-03-03). A pipeline that fails
on everything is not a gate — it is a broken pipeline that happens to look strict.

## The trap

`revenue > 0` passes the €0 case and misses the point entirely. **€12 is also
implausible for twelve supermarkets on a Wednesday.** The bounds in your team
config exist precisely so that a hardcoded threshold is visibly wrong.

And they are *your team's* bounds. A neighbour's range will not fit your data.

## Where the layers are

The sharpest boundary in the course, and worth arguing about:

`revenue < min_plausible` is **processing** when it computes a boolean, and
**orchestration** when that boolean decides whether the world is told the dataset
exists.

Same expression. Two layers. Distinguished only by who consumes the answer.

- **Orchestration:** where the gate sits, that it stops the run, and that the
  asset event is conditional on it.
- **Processing:** computing the total and comparing it to the bounds.

## Think about this before the debrief

**What exactly did you make Airflow treat as success — and who decided that
definition, you or the tool?**

Then: *on 2026-03-01 the old pipeline was green and wrong. Would your pipeline
have caught it? What would Finance have seen instead?*
