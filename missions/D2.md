# D2 — Validity as part of the definition

**20 minutes.** This is the load-bearing Dagster mission.

## Where we are

You can see the €0 in the catalog.

Seeing it is not the same as stopping it.

## What this mission is about

Expressing a data contract as a **declared, named, blocking property of a
dataset** — and then comparing that honestly with the gate you hand-built in A4.

## Your task

1. **Write an asset check** on `daily_revenue` asserting the revenue total falls
   within `plausible_daily_revenue_eur` from `config/team.yaml`.

2. **Make it blocking**, with `ERROR` severity, and attach the observed figure as
   check metadata.

3. **Re-materialize.** Confirm the check fails *and* that downstream work is
   genuinely prevented.

4. **Fetch the corrected 2026-03-04 re-delivery** and confirm the check passes.
   Re-materializing `raw_delivery` will pick it up once it is available to you.

## What success looks like

Step 3: `daily_revenue` materializes, but its check fails — visibly, against the
asset, with the observed total in the metadata — and downstream materialization
is blocked.

Step 4: the corrected delivery lands and the check goes green.

```
roots verify d2
```

`check_failing_correctly` and `check_passing` both require real behaviour. The
check must fail on the €0 data *and* pass on the corrected data. Without both,
`return AssetCheckResult(passed=False)` would earn the milestone, which would
teach nothing.

## Things worth discovering rather than being told

- **Omitting `blocking=True`** leaves you with a check that fails while
  downstream work proceeds anyway. Try it. That is a *notification*, not a gate,
  and the difference is the mission.
- **`WARN` instead of `ERROR`** has the same consequence.
- **Putting the check logic inside the asset and raising** reproduces your A4
  design exactly — and forfeits the entire lesson. If you find yourself doing
  this, stop and ask what the check is *for*.
- **`> 0`** passes the corrected delivery and would have passed a €12 day. Again.

## Where the layers are

- **Orchestration:** `blocking=True` — the decision that invalidity stops
  downstream work.
- **Processing:** the bounds comparison. Character-for-character the same
  predicate as A4.
- **Boundary:** the check's *declaration* against the asset. In A4 this rule was
  a task that happened to raise. Here it is a named property of the dataset with
  its own recorded result.
