# A1 — Establish contact

**15 minutes.**

## Where we are

Finance is still angry about 2026-03-01. You saw the dashboard at the start of
the session: every store at €0.00, and a completely green log above it.

Before anyone can fix that, there has to be a pipeline to fix. Your first job is
unglamorous: get yesterday's delivery out of SupplyHub and onto disk, under
Airflow.

## What this mission is about

Orchestration is mostly about coordinating systems the orchestrator does not own.
SupplyHub is someone else's service, with someone else's uptime and someone
else's idea of what a helpful response looks like. Airflow's job is to talk to
it, record what happened, and let something else depend on the result.

Also: credentials belong outside pipeline code. You will see why the first time
someone asks you to share the repo.

## Your task

1. **Create an Airflow connection** named `supplyhub`.

   ```
   roots airflow-conn
   ```

   prints the exact values to type into **Admin → Connections** in the Airflow
   UI. Read what it prints — one of the fields is a common trap.

2. **Complete `fetch_delivery`** in `dags/rootsmarkt_delivery.py` so it asks
   SupplyHub for business date **2026-03-02** and lands the file on disk.

   `source.client_from_airflow_conn(SUPPLYHUB_CONN_ID)` gives you a client.
   You do not need to write any HTTP code.

   The token must come from the connection. Not from a literal in the DAG, not
   from an environment variable you added.

3. **Unpause the DAG and trigger it.** It is paused by default; a paused DAG that
   does nothing looks exactly like a broken one.

## What success looks like

One DAG run, one green task, one CSV on disk of roughly 4,800 rows across 12
stores. The task log shows the delivery ID and the row count it landed.

```
roots verify a1
```

should report `connected` and `raw_delivery_landed`.

## Where the layers are

- **Orchestration:** the `@dag`/`@task` wiring and the connection lookup. *This file.*
- **Processing:** everything inside `source.py`. *Sealed, already written.*
- **Boundary:** the output path. It is the contract the next task depends on —
  which is why the helper `delivery_file()` exists rather than you choosing one.

## Think about this before the debrief

You could have written this as three lines of `requests.get` in a cron job.

**Name two things Airflow now knows about this fetch that those three lines
would not have.**
