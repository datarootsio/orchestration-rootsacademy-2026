# D4 — The comparison

**15 minutes.**

## Where we are

You have now solved the same problem twice, two different ways.

You are the only people in the room qualified to compare them.

## What this mission is about

Producing trade-off reasoning grounded in code **you wrote**, not in anything you
were told. This milestone counts exactly as much as the implementation ones — the
board cannot be topped on typing speed.

## Your task

Open your A4 DAG and your D2 check side by side. Then answer four questions, one
or two sentences each.

1. **Your three 30-second answers, collated.** (Where the €0 lived; how you would
   find failing datasets in six months; what would have told you `raw_delivery`
   did not need re-running.) **What single difference do they point at?**

2. **A new colleague joins on Monday** and must add a `weekly_revenue` dataset.
   Which implementation do they understand faster, and why?

3. **Name one thing that was genuinely more work, or genuinely worse, in
   Dagster.**

4. **You are handed a pipeline** that sends a report email, triggers an external
   job, and refreshes a dashboard. Which model fits it better, and why?

Submit with:

```
roots submit d4
```

## On question 3

It is the hardest and it matters most. **Answers that dodge it are returned.**

"Nothing, Dagster was better" is the outcome this mission exists to prevent. If
that is genuinely what you think, then the question is: what would have to be
true of a pipeline for the other tool to win? Answer that instead.

There are real answers here. A few directions worth considering: how obvious is a
dependency to someone who has never seen the file? How do you orchestrate
something that is not a dataset at all? What does a run-centric view give you
across two hundred pipelines that an asset catalog does not?

## One honest caveat, stated up front

**The second implementation of anything is easier.** Some of the smoothness of
the Dagster half is that, and some of it is that the problem was chosen to suit
asset checks. Neither is a fact about the tools. Factor it out of your answers.

## Where the layers are

Neither. This is engineering judgement, which is the actual objective of the day.
