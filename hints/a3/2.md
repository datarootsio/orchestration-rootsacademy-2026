**A3 — hint 2 of 2**

`PostgresOperator` was **removed in Airflow 3**. Every AI assistant trained on
Airflow 2 material will suggest it anyway.

Use `SQLExecuteQueryOperator` from `airflow.providers.common.sql.operators.sql`,
and note that its `schema` argument is now called `database`.

`ensure_warehouse_table` in your DAG is already written in the correct form —
copy its shape.

On dependencies: calling a task and passing the result wires both ordering *and*
data. For ordering with no data, use `>>`. `ensure_warehouse_table` needs the
second kind.
