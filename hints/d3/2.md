**D3 — hint 2 of 2**

Dagster will happily rebuild everything for you. Before you let it: what does
re-materializing `raw_delivery` actually *do*?

Would you want that running against a supplier's API every time you fix a typo?

In the UI, select the specific assets you want and materialize those — or select
`clean_sales` and use the downstream selection. Not **Materialize all**.

The filter itself goes in your `clean_sales` asset, against `cfg.expected_stores`
— never a hardcoded `["T-999"]`.
