**A5 — hint 2 of 2**

Declare the `Asset` again in the new file. Do not import it from
`rootsmarkt_delivery` — importing across DAG files re-executes that module,
registering its DAG a second time, and Airflow rejects the duplicate.

Assets are matched by name and URI, so two independent declarations with the same
values refer to the same asset.

To read the metadata that triggered you, look at `triggering_asset_events` in the
task context.
